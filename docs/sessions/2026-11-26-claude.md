# 2026-11-26 — Claude: keys, joystick, keypad, pin 99

## Changed
- The left-arrow key gives `_` (travel) on both machines; RUN/STOP stays ESC
  (`src/hal/sys.c`, `sys128.c`; Jolyon, 2026-11-25).
- C128 input (`src/hal/sys128.c`):
  - the matrix scan reads the 24 extra keys on `$D02F`. The keypad moves as a PC keypad does
    (5 searches), and so do the cursor keys above it. ESC is ESC, HELP is `?`.
  - a joystick in port 2 gives the eight moves. Fire with a direction is the rush, fire alone
    `s`. There is no repeat. Contacts settle for 30 ms on CIA 1's timer B.
- Self-test check 9: the keyboard buffer's PETSCII on both machines, and the C128's decoder on
  made-up scans.
- Pin 99 (ADR-0007): seven fixes from the far fork with five new tests. The far-spaces series
  moved onto it (22 commits, the same compiler sources as far31). The fork's old head is
  tagged `far-spaces-on-pin87`.
- `docs/TO-DECIDE.md`: the stick, the keypad, the pin additions and the rebase (not now) are
  decided. The fire button and repeat are left open, as built.
- `docs/data/bankplan-2026-11-26-c128fc50.json`: `decode`, `settle` and `joy_keys` go in
  bank 55.
